Marilyst / Session list concept · illustrative data

One row, read top to bottom.

Title and time share the first line. The summary sits under it. Everything else is one quiet line that only appears when it has something to say. Nothing floats above the title, and nothing is drawn under the row.

What each mark means

Bold title
Unread. New output since you last opened it.
Needs you
A question, plan or permission is waiting, in this session or one under it.
Running
Working now. Shown on the session that is working; the parent says how many.
13 sessions
Everything nested under this one, at any depth. Replaces the number and robot icon.
23 uncommitted
Files this session edited that are not committed yet.
Context 86%
Shown only from 80%, amber, then red from 90%. Below that it is in the tooltip. Replaces the bar under every row, which read as progress.
Next / Blocked
The agent’s own one-line note about what remains.

What changed from today’s list

Section labels
Running, Needs you and Idle are small labels, not headings larger than the titles. Needs you comes first because it is the only one asking for action.
Expand arrow
Sits beside the title it opens, not in a far-left column.
Nested sessions
One line each: mark, title, time. The first five show, the rest are one click away.
Long titles
Wrap to two lines before truncating; the full title is in the tooltip.
Toolbar and menus
Filter, new session and options stay beside search. The right-click menu and hover details are unchanged; a pin shows after the title.
Time
Short form (3m, 4h) on the title line, right-aligned so it forms a column.
